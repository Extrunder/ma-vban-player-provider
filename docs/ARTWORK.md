# Provider artwork

The provider icons combine official Music Assistant and VBAN Protocol artwork.
They identify the supported projects; this community provider is independently maintained.

- Music Assistant: the two icon paths from the official frontend's
  [src/assets/icon.svg](https://github.com/music-assistant/frontend/blob/main/src/assets/icon.svg),
  as distributed with the tested Music Assistant server. Original colours and proportions
  are retained; SVG editor metadata and unrelated wordmark paths are omitted.
- VBAN Protocol: the official transparent
  [black logo](https://voicemeeter.com/wp-content/uploads/vban-protocol-logo-black-480x227.png)
  and [white logo](https://voicemeeter.com/wp-content/uploads/vban-protocol-logo-white-480x227.png)
  from the [VB-Audio resources page](https://voicemeeter.com/vb-audio-download-logos-sounds-wallpapers-overlays-promotion-material/).
  They are scaled proportionally and embedded as PNG in the SVG so each icon fits
  the community catalog's 5 KB limit and needs no external image requests.

The Music Assistant mark sits above the VBAN Protocol logo with no overlap.
The light theme uses the black VBAN logo; the dark theme uses the official white variant.
